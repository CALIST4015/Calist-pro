Pre-Production

Pre-production prepares a project before creative work begins. We clarify the purpose and audience, discuss the creative direction, define the project requirements, and plan an approach that fits the service and scope.

What happens

  1. Clarify the project goals, audience, intended use, and what the work needs to communicate.
  2. Discuss the creative direction, including visual tone, references, and any relevant existing brand material.
  3. Define the project requirements, scope, expected outputs, and any practical constraints.
  4. Plan a workflow and timeline around the agreed requirements and project context.
  5. Prepare service-specific materials where useful, such as concepts, scripts, shot lists, schedules, or design references.

Useful outcomes

  • A shared understanding of the project goals, audience, and creative direction.
  • Defined requirements and an agreed scope to guide the creative work.
  • A planned approach and timeline suited to the project.
  • Concepts, scripts, shot lists, schedules, or design references when they are relevant to the service.

Specific preparation materials depend on the project; not every item applies to every service.

Across our services

Preparation is shaped around the creative work being planned.

Photography

Consider the subject, setting, visual direction, image use, and any relevant shot planning or session logistics.

Videography

Shape the story and format, and consider scripts, scenes, locations, schedules, and production needs where applicable.

Motion Graphics

Clarify the message and intended use, then explore a visual style, references, and the assets or information needed.

Graphic Design

Define the audience, message, format, visual direction, and relevant brand references or usage requirements.

Every project starts with a clear direction.
