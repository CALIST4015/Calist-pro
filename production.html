Production

Production is where the planned creative work is made. We put the agreed direction and project requirements into practice, adapting the working process to the service and the needs of the project.

What happens

  1. Use the project direction, requirements, and relevant preparation materials to guide the work.
  2. Create the agreed creative work: photograph or film, design visual assets, or produce motion graphics.
  3. Coordinate the practical details involved in making the work, as appropriate to the service and scope.
  4. Keep the work aligned with the project goals and address production needs as they arise.

Useful outcomes

  • Creative material made in line with the agreed direction and project requirements.
  • Captured images or footage, design compositions, motion sequences, or other working materials as relevant.
  • Production observations or decisions that help guide the refinement stage.

The form and extent of work created depend on the agreed project scope and service.

Across our services

The making process varies with the medium while staying connected to the agreed direction.

Photography

Photograph people, products, places, or moments, guided by the planned visual approach and image requirements.

Videography

Film the planned scenes and supporting material, shaping the footage around the intended story and use.

Motion Graphics

Build and animate visual elements to communicate the intended message in the planned style.

Graphic Design

Create layouts and visual assets that follow the agreed message, format, and design direction.

Thoughtful making brings the direction to life.
